Use of the apps

Page Sorter changes the order of pages in your Confluence page tree when you, or an auto-sort rule you created, ask it to. It doesn't change page content.

Draft Cleaner permanently deletes the unpublished drafts you select and confirm. Confluence does not keep deleted drafts in the trash, so deletions can't be undone. The app never deletes published pages.
