How to use Page Sorter

  1. Open a Confluence page that has child pages.
  2. Click ••• → Apps → Sort child pages.
  3. Pick an order (A → Z or Z → A) and check the preview.
  4. Click Sort now. Tick Also sort all sub-pages to sort every level.
  5. Click Turn on auto-sort to keep the pages sorted as they're added, renamed or moved.

Space admins can sort a whole space and manage auto-sort rules in Space settings → Page Sorter.

FAQ

Why didn't a page move? You need edit permission on the pages. Folders, whiteboards and databases keep their place; pages are sorted around them.

Large trees? Big folders and "all sub-pages" sorts run in the background. Refresh the page tree after a minute.

Does sorting create new page versions? No. Only the position in the tree changes, and only pages that are out of order are moved.

Sprint Date JQL Functions for Jira

Use these functions anywhere JQL works: issue search, filters, boards, dashboards and automation.

  • issue in sprintsStartedBetween("2026-01-01", "2026-03-31") – issues in sprints that started in that range
  • issue in sprintsEndedBetween("-30d", "today") – sprints whose planned end date is in that range
  • issue in sprintsCompletedBetween("-3m", "today") – sprints actually completed in that range
  • issue in sprintsActiveOn("2026-03-15") – sprints that were running on that day
  • Use not in for the opposite, and combine with anything, e.g. project = ABC AND ...

Dates: exact days (2026-03-31), today, or relative days such as -14d, -2w, -3m, -1y. Ranges include both days and use UTC.
First use: the app loads your sprint list in the background after installation; on very large sites the first search may ask you to try again a minute later.
Status and refresh: Jira settings → Apps → Sprint Date JQL Functions. Sprint changes are picked up automatically and a full refresh runs daily.
