Privacy policy

Applies to: Sort Pages Alphabetically for Confluence (Page Sorter). Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Summary

Page Sorter runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud (Atlassian Forge, "Runs on Atlassian"). We, Tidyleaf, don't operate any servers for it and we can't see your Confluence content.

What the app reads

To sort pages, the app reads page titles, page IDs and the page-tree structure of the pages you choose to sort, using Atlassian's official APIs. It uses this data only to work out and apply the new order.

What the app stores

Only your auto-sort settings: the page ID, space ID, page title, chosen sort order, whether sub-pages are included, your locale, and the date the rule was created. They're stored in Atlassian's Forge storage, inside Atlassian's cloud. The app does not store page content, personal data or account IDs.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share or send your data to third parties, and the app doesn't send data outside Atlassian's cloud. We use no tracking or analytics of our own.

Deleting your data

Turning off auto-sort deletes that rule. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data according to Atlassian's Forge data-retention policies.

Atlassian

Atlassian's handling of data is covered by the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
