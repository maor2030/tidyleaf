Privacy policy

Applies to all Tidyleaf apps: Sort Pages Alphabetically for Confluence (Page Sorter) and Draft Cleaner for Confluence. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Summary

Tidyleaf apps run entirely on Atlassian's cloud (Atlassian Forge, "Runs on Atlassian"). We, Tidyleaf, don't operate any servers for them and we can't see your Confluence content.

Page Sorter

Reads: page titles, page IDs and the page-tree structure of the pages you choose to sort, through Atlassian's official APIs, only to work out and apply the new order.

Stores: only your auto-sort settings (page ID, space ID, page title, sort order, whether sub-pages are included, your locale and the date the rule was created), in Atlassian's Forge storage inside Atlassian's cloud. It does not store page content, personal data or account IDs.

Draft Cleaner

Reads: the list of your own unpublished drafts (title, space and last-edited date), through Atlassian's official APIs, to show them to you.

Stores: nothing. The app keeps no data at all. Drafts you delete are deleted by Confluence at your request.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share or send your data to third parties, and our apps don't send data outside Atlassian's cloud. We use no tracking or analytics of our own.

Deleting your data

In Page Sorter, turning off auto-sort deletes that rule. Uninstalling an app removes its stored data according to Atlassian's Forge data-retention policies. Draft Cleaner stores no data.

Atlassian

Atlassian's handling of data is covered by the Atlassian Privacy Policy.
